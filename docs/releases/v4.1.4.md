# Flight Commander 4.1.4

Flight Commander 4.1.4 replaces the withdrawn 4.1.3 official release with a
coordinated Configurator 4.1.4 and MICOAIR743 Firmware 4.1.4 package.

## Highlights

- Removes the connected-firmware `FCFW` authorization gate. A live Flight
  Commander MSP or MAVLink connection is usable without waiting for a version
  signature, capability bitmap, or probe timeout. A cached aircraft profile is
  used only for its aircraft-specific AUX/mode mapping, not firmware approval.
- Enables the complete Flight Commander feature contract for every connected
  Flight Commander release. Optional identity and capability fields remain
  visible as diagnostic metadata but cannot disable configuration, mission,
  RTK, or Ground Control routes.
- Keeps link state, arming state, aircraft-specific AUX/mode mapping, mission
  validation, and target selection as operational safety checks; none of those
  checks attempt to classify the firmware family.
- Describes LTM accurately as a telemetry-only transport and directs mission
  and command use to MAVLink.
- Preserves operator-controlled local HEX flashing and the official/beta-only
  online firmware catalog introduced in 4.1.3.

## Official assets

`Flight-Commander-v4.1.4.zip` contains exactly:

- `FC-Windows-v4.1.4.zip`
- `FC-Configurator-Source-v4.1.4.zip`
- `FC-Firmware-v4.1.4-MICOAIR743.hex`
- `FC-Firmware-Source-v4.1.4.zip`

The standalone `Flight-Commander-Firmware-4.1.4-MICOAIR743.hex` is also
published for the Configurator's online flasher. Publication verifies both
assets before the superseded public v4.1.3 release and tag are removed.
