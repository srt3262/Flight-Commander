# Flight Commander 4.3.1

Flight Commander 4.3.1 is a coordinated Configurator and Firmware release for
MICOAIR743 and CubePilot Cube Orange+. The Configurator and both official
firmware targets share the exact 4.3.1 version.

## Configurator corrections

- **Firmware Features** now renders learned compass orientation with the same
  live Supported/Disabled badge and reason text as every other capability.
- **GPS** keeps the converted timezone control in its intended grid column and
  removes the redundant `hh:mm` overlay from the already formatted value.
- **Blackbox** lists serial, dataflash, and microSD storage classes
  consistently. Dataflash remains disabled and is marked not fitted when the
  connected firmware has no real dataflash backend.

## Relative-altitude telemetry

Whenever the aircraft is disarmed, Firmware 4.3.1 reports calibrated barometer
motion to MSP and MAVLink ground-station relative-altitude fields. Every
disarmed-to-armed transition captures the current fused navigation estimate as
a fresh display datum, so relative altitude reads zero at arming and tracks the
normal fused estimate from that point.

This makes Status, Sensors, Flight Data, and Ground Control altitude indicators
respond while the controller is moved vertically during propeller-off testing
and reset predictably for every flight, without changing RTH, altitude hold,
waypoint, or landing control inputs.

## Blackbox storage contract

- MICOAIR743 records onboard logs to its SDMMC1 microSD slot.
- Cube Orange+ also records onboard logs to SDMMC1. Its removable microSD slot
  is beneath the USB connector.
- Cube Orange+ FRAM is configuration storage, not a high-rate Blackbox medium;
  neither official target exposes MCU program flash as a logging filesystem.
- Empty SDMMC slots on boards without a physical card-detect switch are now
  reported as unavailable instead of as a fatal card error.
- Firmware now reports compiled dataflash support independently from temporary
  chip readiness, so future targets with a real backend can expose it correctly.

## Verified release

Both official images are rebuilt with pinned Arm GNU 13.2.1 and warnings as
errors. Publication verifies their embedded `FCFW` 4.3.1 identity,
`0x0001ffff` capability mask, exact target, protected address range, source
identity, byte count, and SHA-256.

`Flight-Commander-v4.3.1.zip` contains exactly:

- `FC-Windows-v4.3.1.zip`
- `FC-Configurator-Source-v4.3.1.zip`
- `FC-Firmware-v4.3.1-MICOAIR743.hex`
- `FC-Firmware-v4.3.1-CUBEORANGEPLUS.hex`
- `FC-Firmware-Source-v4.3.1.zip`

The two firmware images are also published as standalone online-flasher assets
under their canonical long filenames.
