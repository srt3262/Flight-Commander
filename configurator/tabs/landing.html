Unified configuration, mission planning, and ground control

Configure and fly from one application

Configure Flight Commander aircraft through the wired MSP connection, then use MAVLink telemetry for airborne ground control. Monitor live telemetry, send supported commands, and plan missions through one Flight Commander Firmware-only application and release contract.

Mission and mapping tools

Build waypoint missions, generate survey grids from polygons, apply terrain-following altitudes, and transfer active missions over MAVLink. Wired MSP remains available for persistent mission storage and configuration.

Flight Commander capabilities

Configure the aircraft, manage firmware, plan missions, and operate live Ground Control from one coordinated application. The Ground Control workspace combines satellite mapping, a real-time attitude HUD, telemetry, mission progress, flight-mode control, launch, return-home, landing, abort, and same-session mission resume.

Build waypoint and polygon survey missions with terrain-following altitudes, camera triggering, speed and completion policies, then transfer and verify the active route. USB RTK base workflows, native NTRIP, UART and DroneCAN GNSS, dual-GNSS moving-baseline yaw, and weighted heading fusion are integrated into the same workflow.

Flight Commander also provides sensor calibration, receiver and mode setup, failsafe behavior, motor and servo outputs, tuning, OSD, blackbox and tethered logging, programming, and peripheral configuration for the supported aircraft platform.

Open-source Flight Commander

Flight Commander is independently versioned, developed in public, and licensed under GPL-3.0. Its Configurator and flight-controller firmware are maintained together as one aircraft-control platform.
