Flight Commander 4.0.6

Flight Commander 4.0.6 Beta introduces a learned onboard-compass orientation workflow for the MICOAIR743. The firmware no longer assumes a fixed signed-axis transform for the IST8310.

Complete the six-position accelerometer calibration first, then use the guided six-face compass-orientation procedure. Rotate the entire controller around the requested upward face until each position is complete. Firmware compares synchronized accelerometer, gyro and canonical magnetometer vectors across all 24 physically valid right-handed axis mappings, rejects ambiguous data, and permanently stores the winning transform with its confidence and residual.

The learned sensor transform is independent from manual compass alignment and board alignment. Changing the learned transform clears the previous magnetic offsets and gains, after which the normal compass field calibration must be completed. Heading fusion excludes the onboard compass until both stages are valid.

The managed flasher offers the verified 3.0.7 recovery baseline plus 4.0.6 and later. Failed 3.0.6-and-earlier, 3.0.8 and 4.0.0-through-4.0.5 firmware remain excluded from the managed list.
