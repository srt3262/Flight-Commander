Flight Commander automatically lists every enabled compass it can currently detect. Start from any compass card, then rotate the complete aircraft slowly through every orientation for about 30 seconds.

The flight controller calibrates all enabled physical compasses during the same rotation session. Each card refreshes from its own onboard, external, or CAN calibration storage when the session ends.
