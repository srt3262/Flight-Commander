# Flight Commander 4.0.7 Beta

Flight Commander 4.0.7 is a coordinated Configurator and MICOAIR743 firmware hotfix for the mirrored-roll regression reported in 4.0.6.

## IMU handedness correction

Firmware 4.0.6 added separate pre-board-alignment accelerometer and gyro buffers inside the live IMU update loops for learned compass orientation. On the Aero Selfie H743, the reported attitude could then become left/right mirrored.

4.0.7 removes every added buffer write from both high-rate IMU paths and restores the verified 4.0.5 processing sequence:

1. Apply the fixed BMI088 sensor alignment.
2. Apply the saved board alignment.
3. Publish the attitude vector used by the estimator and telemetry.

The compass-orientation learner still receives calibrated pre-board-alignment vectors, but now derives them on demand from the latest raw samples only when the 40 Hz compass task requests them. The target remains `IMU_BMI088_ALIGN CW270_DEG`; no firmware-side HUD workaround or user alignment change is used.

## Hardware bench result

Propeller-off testing of the 4.0.7 firmware confirmed that learned compass orientation completes successfully, the displayed magnetic heading is correct, yaw moves smoothly with heading, and compass sample quality remains above 90 percent while the controller is stationary. The Status-tab 3D model also follows the controller correctly on roll, pitch and yaw.

That testing isolated two remaining Configurator presentation defects rather than a firmware attitude or compass-transform error:

- The artificial-horizon renderer applied a second roll inversion even though MSP attitude already used the expected aircraft-frame handedness. The redundant inversion has been removed.
- The guided compass-orientation display labeled firmware face `+Y` as right-side-up and `-Y` as left-side-up. Only those human-readable labels were exchanged; the firmware face indices, learned vectors and stored transform are unchanged.

Focused regression coverage now requires the artificial horizon and Status 3D scene to use their correct complementary roll rotations and requires `+Y`/`-Y` to display as left-side-up/right-side-up respectively.

## Retained functionality

The persistent learned IST8310 sensor-to-board transform, independent manual CW0 compass alignment, conventional compass field calibration, stationary heading availability, startup yaw initialization and moving-baseline RTK support remain enabled.

The managed online flasher offers the verified 3.0.7 recovery baseline and firmware 4.0.7 or later. Firmware 4.0.6 is excluded because of the IMU handedness regression.

## Remaining propeller-off acceptance check

Use the updated 4.0.7 Configurator with the propellers removed and verify:

- Tilt the controller with its left side up, then with its right side up. For both movements, the artificial horizon must indicate the same physical roll sense as the already-correct Status 3D model.
- During compass-orientation relearning, physically raising the left side must display **Left side up**, and physically raising the right side must display **Right side up**.
- Raise the nose and rotate the controller through yaw once more to confirm those already-correct axes remain unchanged.

The firmware and compass-orientation result have passed the reported bench check. Complete this final Configurator presentation check before any armed or flight test.
