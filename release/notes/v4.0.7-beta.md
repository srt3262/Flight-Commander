# Flight Commander 4.0.7 Beta

Flight Commander 4.0.7 is a coordinated Configurator and MICOAIR743 firmware hotfix for the mirrored-roll regression reported in 4.0.6.

## IMU handedness correction

Firmware 4.0.6 added separate pre-board-alignment accelerometer and gyro buffers inside the live IMU update loops for learned compass orientation. On the Aero Selfie H743, the reported attitude could then become left/right mirrored: physically raising the right side displayed the left side rising on the HUD.

4.0.7 removes every added buffer write from both high-rate IMU paths and restores the verified 4.0.5 processing sequence:

1. Apply the fixed BMI088 sensor alignment.
2. Apply the saved board alignment.
3. Publish the attitude vector used by the estimator and telemetry.

The compass-orientation learner still receives calibrated pre-board-alignment vectors, but now derives them on demand from the latest raw samples only when the 40 Hz compass task requests them. The target remains `IMU_BMI088_ALIGN CW270_DEG`; no HUD-only sign inversion or user alignment workaround is used.

## Retained functionality

The persistent learned IST8310 sensor-to-board transform, independent manual CW0 compass alignment, conventional compass field calibration, stationary heading availability, startup yaw initialization and moving-baseline RTK support remain enabled.

The managed online flasher now offers the verified 3.0.7 recovery baseline and firmware 4.0.7 or later. Firmware 4.0.6 is excluded because of the IMU handedness regression.

## Propeller-off acceptance check

After flashing, keep the propellers removed and recalibrate the accelerometer. Verify all three attitude directions before arming:

- Raise the controller's right side: the HUD right side must rise.
- Raise the nose: the HUD nose must rise.
- Rotate clockwise when viewed from above: yaw and heading must move in the corresponding direction.

Then complete learned compass orientation and the conventional compass field calibration. This beta requires physical target-hardware acceptance before flight.
